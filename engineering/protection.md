# Protection and failure behavior

This is an indoor low-voltage prototype. The protected sealed1S battery power assembly is defined in POWER_SYSTEM.md; its modified boost and external latching breaker deliver regulated5V to J_PWR. No raw cell connects to the carrier. Q1 is reversed relative to a high-side switch: input on D5–8, protected LOAD on S1–3, gate on GND. Positive input forward-biases its body diode initially then turns the channel on; negative input leaves VGS≈0 and the diode reverse biased. Q2 source is LOAD, drain SERVO, gate pulled to source by10k. USB logic positive and load positive have no copper connection. Shared ground is intentional.

The7A fuse protects against a high-current wiring source; it is not a6A electronic limiter. A6A foldback bench source may never clear this fuse. The battery assembly uses a separate latching electronic breaker qualified to trip at6.1–7.2A with zero programmed blanking; F1 is backup. Source fault behavior, input wire gauge, copper thermal margin and no-load short testing remain acceptance requirements. Never substitute an automotive battery or high-current unregulated supply. Individual servo leads must not be extended with thin unknown wire. Disconnect power before changing any plug.

AHCT trigger inputs have no positive VCC clamp per their absolute input specification, so3V3 inputs with LOAD absent do not have a diode feed path. Echo uses5.5V-tolerant LVC Schmitt inputs with Ioff while logic is off. All signal connections are inside the chassis; external cables and hot-plug abuse are outside this prototype rating. HC-SR04P modules must be connected unpowered with VCC/TRIG/ECHO/GND order checked.

PWM isolation and default-off proof are in power.md. A running MCU stuck at ACT_EN high is not detected by discrete hardware; firmware watchdog/control timeout must reset or lower it. No functional-safety certification is claimed. STOP and controller loss require firmware and independent integration tests; electrical reset alone is hardware enforced.

Q2's body diode can return motor energy into LOAD_5V while on or off. C1 reduces spikes but is not an overvoltage clamp. No absorption ability is assumed for the battery boost/breaker assembly. At bringup scope LOAD/SERVO during starts/stops/back-drive; accept≤5.5V. If exceeded, the board team must add a characterized regenerative clamp or qualify a sink-capable5V supply before mechanical drive trials. Do not infer a safe regen-energy bound from an unmeasured wheel. TVS parts whose advertised5V standoff clamps near9V were deliberately not presented as5.5V protection.

J_FAN is a two-terminal output directly from protected LOAD_5V andGND, ahead of the servo switch. It is protected by F1, reverse Q1 and the external latching breaker, and runs regardless of servo ACT_EN. Use a short>=22AWG insulated pair; allocation<=0.30A including startup. No third control wire or firmware GPIO.

V0.3 pack-fault repair: F_PACK=Littelfuse0287015.U15A/32VDC/1kA in0FHA0002XP30A12AWG holder at sealed-pack positive takeoff. Additional input M_PACK_BREAKER (second TPS259824OEVM) precedes both boost inputs,qualified12.8–14.8A zero-blanking latch-off; it closes the moderate-overload gap of the65±10A protector. Pack cell/interconnect and12AWG harness fault coordination and maximum prospective100A are specified in POWER_SYSTEM.md and pack-protection-spec.json. These limits require qualified closed-pack evidence; native pass does not verify battery safety.
