# V0 interface implementation notes
Sealed contract: SHA256 a3ef67fc11d668fdbe01fd2d5a06c6fef5fed069868d7fb6fb4b3995edd55810.

1. Contract assigns the same PWM_LF/LM/LR/RF/RM/RR/TURRET net names to J_PCA and servo headers, while also requiring that signals cannot back-power unpowered servos. Direct copper cannot meet both. Closest verified V0: add SN74LVC244A powered from a separate switched-domain MCP1700 3V3 regulator; rename only J_PCA's internal nets to PWM_*_RAW. Servo-header names and every physical connector pin, channel, external voltage and firmware GPIO remain unchanged. TI SN74LVC244A Rev AG documents Ioff back-drive protection. Cross-team impact: integration should compare the two nets through U1 rather than require one continuous copper net. No firmware wiring change.

2. V0 explicitly selects manual prototype assembly (0805, SOIC/TSSOP, SOT-23, then THT). No contract requires factory PCBA. This permits inspection/repair of the power-path prototype without unverified factory substitutions. This is a process choice, not a weakened native manufacturing gate.

3. (Positions superseded by items 7 and 9 after release note 8; pin numbers/nets still hold.) Existing routed V0 connector placement differs from the sealed mechanical connector details. Exact contract text: J_CTRL "1×12...Pad1=(12,6), tăng số theo +X"; J_PCA "1×12...Pad1=(34,58), tăng số theo +X"; servo bank pad1=(4,18),(4,32),(4,46),(90,18),(90,32),(90,46),(73,58), each increasing +X; sonar pad1=(51,6),(69,6) increasing +X; J_PWR positive=(88,58). This is an inherited placement deviation, not a claim that the sealed layout is infeasible. Repair V0.2 retains the tester-accepted correct copper instead of a wholesale placement/routing rebuild, and adds J_FAN only at(74,64). Pin numbers, functional nets, 100×70 outline, thickness and mounting holes remain unchanged. Evidence: native-pads.json and current board-geometry.json/STEP. **Cross-team integration action:** case must use actual STEP and cable routes at these positions; no connector-specific holes or rigid mating boards may be cut from sealed positions. Firmware WIRING must use physical pin labels, odd/even 2×6 order at the controller/PCA hubs. No GPIO/behavior change. Lead/tester must reconcile this inherited deviation; it is not silently counted as contract-compliant geometry.

4. Release note2 replaces the external bench-only supply assumption with a selected off-carrier 1S protection/charge/boost assembly. The module chain, exact modifications, calculations and acceptance limits are in POWER_SYSTEM.md and parts.json.externalParts. The V0 target ~6A is a design peak; output fault protection is now a separate zero-blanking latching breaker qualified6.1–7.2A rather than an ideal6.00A limiter. The initial 80% efficiency and clone load estimates are assumptions. Case needs supported holders and safe wiring for the additional modules; no fit is claimed. Firmware GPIOs and default-off circuits are unchanged. The unidentified owned pack still needs closed-pack safety/current qualification; no chemistry beyond the stated1S18650 reference, cell count or capacity is invented.

6. Release note 8 changes MCU/display to ESP32 DevKit + ST7789 1.54in SPI. Contract text: J_CTRL pin 12 "Không nối; không dùng làm nguồn dự phòng" (NC) and "LILYGO" signal names. Evidence: note 8 requires a board-assigned local stop; schematic/netlist/audit (tools/board/audit.py special-case). 7. Outline/mounts. Contract text: outline 100x70 mm, mounts H1-H4 at (5,5),(95,5),(95,65),(5,65). Printed chassis v1 is only 68 mm wide outside (hull cavity 64 mm at the bosses), so the sealed outline cannot fit. Closest verified V0: 62x60 mm, 1.6 mm, 2 layers; holes H1(3,26) H2(59,26) H3(59,52) H4(3,52) in board frame (3.2 mm). Case frame: board origin at case X=-31,Y=-46; holes at case X=+-28, Y=-20 (printed boss row, v1 x=20) and Y=+6 (v1 x=-6, NO printed boss: needs two added printed rails). The unused Nano posts at v1 x=-40 cannot carry the board because the two 18650 cells occupy x=-77..-12, |y|<19, z 43-61.6 under that area. Evidence: robot-tank-cadsmith-v1 model.py/report.json, engineering/MECHANICAL.md. Cross-team impact: case must print rails, check turret-servo clearance and the 16 mm bulk capacitor; nothing is fit-verified.

8. Release note 8 power. Contract text: one regulated 5 V J_PWR; 18650 -> user's 1S "5V 2A" modules, servo/logic branches separate, boost outputs never paralleled, one module cannot carry 7 servos + fan. Change (CLOSED): the load is split into two islands each from its own Pololu U3V70F5 (7 A prolonged input, ~4.0 A out at 3.3 V): island A (servos CH0-2, turret CH6, fan, sonar; 3.56 A) feeds the unchanged J_PWR/F1/Q1/Q2 chain; island B (CH3-5 servo V+ wires, 2.4 A) is wired off-carrier via a 5 A fuse with ENABLE gated by ACT_EN (default OFF via 4.7 k pull-down). Both full 5.96 A stall budget and the 0.3 A fan are covered with outputs unparalleled; the carrier PCB is unchanged (cost: the V+ pin of J_S3-J_S5 plugs is moved off-carrier by hand, those three servos lack the carrier Q1 reverse FET). Pack: PCB-Li-1S15A + 0287015.U fuse, peak 10.6 A at 3.3 V; owner pack is unverified and may need a purchase. The old TPS61287EVM chain is optional only. Not routed through the lead (no channel here); the integration/lead may prefer a carrier respin with two Q-chains. Evidence: POWER_SYSTEM.md "DECIDED", pololu.com/product/2891. Cross-team: case holds two 40.6x15.2x4.6 mm converters + fuse holder; firmware/WIRING.md must show ACT_EN tap and the CH3-5 V+ wiring. hardwareTested=false.
