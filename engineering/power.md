# Power and reset-state design

V0.2 applies release notes1–2: the user’s140g parallel18650 pack is the1S reference. Selected sealed-pack protection/charging and two boost branches are specified in [POWER_SYSTEM.md](POWER_SYSTEM.md), including exact modules, configuration, wiring, capacity and limitations. J_PWR accepts only regulated4.95–5.25V, never raw1S. The load branch and LILYGO USB branch have separate positive outputs; host mode physically replaces the battery USB cable. LOGIC_3V3 comes only from LILYGO pin3V3. The owned pack is retained as an unqualified assumption; a reference design does not establish its safety.

Reference peak budget:7×0.8A=5.60A servo stall proxy +0.30A fan allocation +0.030A twoHC-SR04P allocation +0.030A switched buffer/bleed =5.96A carrier total. SeparateLILYGO5VUSB allocation0.50A. At3.3V boost input and80% estimated conversion efficiency, the two branches require≈12.64A at the two boost inputs, rounded12.7A at pack; require a qualified sealed pack rated≥15A. See reproducible power-calculations.json and POWER_SYSTEM.md for ordinary-motion/runtime/thermal estimates. No cell count, capacity, fan current or stall measurement is inferred. J_FAN pin1=LOAD_5V,pin2=GND, powered whenever J_PWR is present, independent of ACT_EN; noGPIO is added.

F1→Q1 reverse-polarity MOSFET→LOAD_5V→Q2 high-side MOSFET→SERVO_5V. Each AO4409 has three source pads and four drain pads in parallel. At6A and12mΩ each loses0.432W and72mV at25°C. Use20mΩ as a conservative hot estimate:0.72W and120mV each. Allow≤100mV across fuse/entry copper; predicted motor rail≥4.95−0.10−0.12−0.12=4.61V in the worst hot/stalled estimate. This is a transient envelope, not a promise of rated torque; require≥4.7V during ordinary motion at bench. At the Q2 source the gate drive remains≥4.95−0.10−0.12−0.20=4.53V, within the−4.5V RDS(on) test condition.

C1=1000µF on LOAD, C2/C3=20µF on SERVO plus1µF C7 at U2. For a0.5ms5.6A step,1000µF alone would droop2.8V: the supply/wires must deliver the load, not the capacitor. C1 ESR contribution≈0.157V at5.6A. Do not claim milliseconds of stall hold-up. Capacitor totals and every local IC-pad-to-cap-pad distance are measured from the native board in board-audit.json; the official power scanner does not recognize contract rail names, so its zero-IC result is not evidence of decoupling.

Reset/blank flash/loss of3V3: R4=1k pulls ACT_EN down. Q3's emitter is grounded; with no base drive Q3 is off, R3=10k pulls Q2 gate to its source, Q2 is off. R2=47k keeps Q3 base low. Even a hypothetical20k weak MCU pull-up only raises ACT_EN≈0.157V before R1/R2 loading, far below a forward-biased base. GPIO11 is not a boot-strap pin. There is no RC-loaded shared digital enable.

OE: R5=1k toLOGIC_3V3 overcomes the reference module's10k OE pull-down: VOE=0.909×VCC. With1% carrier resistor and10% module resistor worst ratio9k/(9k+1.01k)=0.899, above PCA VIH0.7×VCC. GPIO10 sinks≈3.3mA when enabling. A clone with<2.4k OE-to-GND requires removing its pulldown before use. This is an assembly qualification step; never assume a bare PCA IC describes the module.

U1 receives raw PCA CH0–6 and is powered by SERVO_3V3, generated only from SERVO_5V through U2. Its Ioff feature blocks signal back-drive when the servo domain is unpowered. Outputs have10k pulldowns;≤10µA off leakage yields≤0.1V on an unloaded signal. Raw inputs have47k pulldowns. U1 OE follows PCA_OE_N; firmware configures PCA MODE2 to output0 or high-Z on OE and leaves CH7–15 off. During logic loss both PCA and raw inputs become low; actual module leakage and servo clones require the staged test.

R6=220Ω2512 bleeds SERVO_5V,≈23mA/0.114W at5V;21µF gives≈4.6ms time constant before unknown servo input capacitance. R7=1k bleeds SERVO_3V3,≈3.3mA/11mW with≈2.1µF. Neither rail discharge is a measured stop time. Servo-domain startup below guaranteed logic voltages is not a commanded pulse; keep OE high throughout the ramp and initialize neutral before enabling. Bench must check no motion/reset transients.
