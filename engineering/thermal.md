Three source pads share≈2A each and four drain pads≈1.5A each at6A. The short0.6mm-wide pad necks are parallel, not a single0.25mm trace carrying6A. Main positive paths are direct-connected70µm pours. For a0.6mm length of0.6mm×0.07mm copper, each2A neck dissipates≈1mW (ρ=0.0172Ω·mm²/m). Long board routing remains signal-level. A small bottom bridge connects only the previously isolated C2 capacitor island; it is not the motor distribution trunk.
